01 / ABOUT

From reliable software to intelligent physical systems.

My work has moved through hardware reliability at Tesla, ML infrastructure at Meta and Apple, high-throughput trading systems at Citadel Securities, and now AI-native engineering at Saphira.

As a founder, I’ve led product direction, built and developed a technical team, and worked directly with engineering organizations to bring AI into their workflows. I’m interested in the path from research capability to useful products: agent tools, evaluation, system context, and deployment.

I’m a University of Washington alumnus with degrees in Computer Science and Mathematics, based in the Seattle area. My interests span AI agents, robotics, semiconductor engineering, and software that interacts with the physical world.
